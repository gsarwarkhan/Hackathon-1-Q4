# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Textbook

Welcome to the interactive textbook for **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics**. This guide covers everything from hardware requirements to advanced robot learning and simulation.

## Getting Started

Use the sidebar on the left to navigate through the chapters. If you have questions while reading, you can click on **"Ask the AI"** in the top navigation bar to speak with our AI assistant trained on this material.

### Topics Covered

- **Hardware Requirements**: Essential workstation specs for simulation and AI.
- **ROS 2 Foundations**: The communication backbone of robotics.
- **Digital Twins**: High-fidelity simulation and physics.
- **Vision-Language-Action Models (VLA)**: The brain of the robot.
